System Architecture

Three layers. One intelligence pipeline.

Built on Google Cloud infrastructure end to end — from ingestion to prediction to the founder-facing report.

01 / Predictive Engine

Failure-risk model

A gradient-boosted classifier trained on historical startup outcome data scores runway, burn multiple, market timing, and team composition against thousands of known failure cases.

02 / Intelligence Layer
